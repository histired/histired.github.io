Maple

Maple is a personal voice assistant that lives on one desktop computer as an animated avatar. You talk to her or type to her, and she answers out loud. She can look things up on the web, plan routes, do date and money maths, and, when asked, check a Google Calendar or send an email.

Personal use only. Maple is not a service and is not offered to the public. She runs entirely on her developer's own computer, using a language model that also runs on that computer. There are no accounts to sign up for and nothing to download here.

Google account access

Maple signs in to her developer's own Google account with Google's standard sign-in, to:

  • Google Calendar: read upcoming events (to answer "what's on tomorrow?" and to give reminders) and add an event, but only after the user has seen it and said yes.
  • Gmail: send an email the user has seen and approved. Maple cannot read any email.

Nothing is written or sent without the user's explicit confirmation. How this data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
